**Venue:** Pulse Grande Hotel, Putrajaya

**Date:** 7 and 8 October 2026

**Time:** From 8:00 AM on Day 1 and 9:00 AM on Day 2 (MYT)

## Event Summary

Organized by the Entrepreneurship Development Centre - International Islamic University Malaysia (EDC-IIUM), IEC 2026 brought together university students, entrepreneurs, industry leaders and investors to connect campus innovation with business opportunities.

Developers Hub participated as one of the sponsors, supporting a platform that introduces students to entrepreneurial thinking, industry perspectives and regional networks.

## Key Insights

### 1. Turning Ideas into Commercial Value

Promising ideas need a clear understanding of customer needs, market demand and business viability. Exposure to pitching and industry feedback gives students opportunities to assess their ideas beyond the classroom.

![](/images/blog/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-10-12%20at%2009.23.54.jpeg)

### 2. Strengthening University and Industry Connections

Engagement between universities and businesses creates opportunities to align talent development with industry needs, explore practical applications and build relationships that can support future collaboration.

![](/images/blog/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-10-12%20at%2009.23.53.jpeg)

### 3. Preparing Founders for Regional Growth

Expanding across ASEAN requires an understanding of different markets, customer expectations and local partnerships. Regional networking provides a starting point for exploring these opportunities.

![](/images/blog/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-10-12%20at%2009.23.37.jpeg)

### 4. Building Foundations for Business Growth

For Developers Hub, participation creates opportunities to connect with emerging talent, understand startup needs and explore potential collaborations in training, product development and digital solutions.

![](/images/blog/WhatsApp%20Image%202026-10-12%20at%2009.23.37%20%281%29.jpeg)

**Developers Hub sees industry engagement as an investment in relationships, talent and future opportunities. Through our support for IEC 2026, we aim to strengthen connections across education, technology and entrepreneurship, creating shared value that supports long-term business growth.**
